Project 1: Images of the Russian Empire

Colorizing the Prokudin-Gorskii Photo Collection | CS 180 (Intro to Computer Vision)

Overview & Approach

Sergei Mikhailovich Prokudin-Gorskii recorded three-color exposures on glass plates across early 20th-century Russia. The goal of this project is to take the digitized glass plate scans, extract the three monochrome color channels (B, G, R), and automatically align them to produce a color photograph with minimal artifacts.

For small images, an exhaustive search over a [-15, 15] displacement window using Sum of Squared Differences (SSD) was implemented. For larger glass plate scans, an image pyramid was used to recursively downscale images, compute coarse offsets cheaply, and iteratively refine displacements at higher resolutions while cropping outer borders to eliminate edge roll artifacts.
